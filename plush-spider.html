Inside the experiment

NOTHING HERE IS QUITE RIGID.
01

Softness, from the inside

A lattice of little cells holds the stuffing together. Shape and volume constraints let it squash, breathe, and gently find its feet again.

02

A wire with a memory

Each sock hides a flexible wire. Bend it past its give and it keeps your gesture. Straighten the legs to return to the original stitch.

03

A thousand tiny threads

Layers of procedural fur catch the light. Their tips lag behind the stuffing, while a brush leaves the pile lying in a new direction.

04

Just enough personality

Four feet lift, four feet stay. A poke becomes a protective curl; a silk thread becomes a swing. Leave it a moment. It may go exploring.

Made of math. Filled with personality.

A gentle introduction.

Hand. Drag the body to lift or squash it. Grab a sock to bend the wire; pull further to tow your companion.

Finger. Move over the table and it will scuttle after you. Touch its body and it curls into a little ball.

Comb. Drag over the fluff to brush it. Smooth fur restores its freshly stitched look.

Right-drag or Alt-drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. On a touch screen, use two fingers to orbit and pinch.
